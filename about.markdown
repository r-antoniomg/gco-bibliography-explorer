The German Canadiana in Ontario Bibliography (GCO) was started in 2007 and is a project of the University of Waterloo Libraries. It is updated continuously, as new information becomes available.

## Scope

* includes documentation relating to German-speaking people and their descendants (where elements of "Germanness" have been identified) in Ontario
* covers aspects such as origin or ancestry, identity, language, social and political history, and religion
* includes subtler dimensions such as culture, folklore, literature, architecture, the arts, and practices such as food preparation
* represents published primary, secondary and tertiary sources, and covers all publication formats
* lists source documents spanning from the late eighteenth century to the present

## Tips for locating items in the GCO

Most items listed in GCO are held by Ontario libraries and archives. Below are suggested sites to help you pinpoint locations. If the item you need is not available locally, request it through interlibrary loan service available at your local library.

### Catalogues:
* [WorldCat](http://www.worldcat.org/)
* [Omni, the library catalogue](https://ocul-wtl.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/search?vid=01OCUL_WTL:WTL_DEFAULT&lang=en&mode=advanced), which includes the holdings of 16 academic libraries in Ontario 
* [Kitchener Public Library's catalogue](http://books.kpl.org/search~S2/)
* [Region of Waterloo Library's catalogue](https://olco.ent.sirsidynix.net/client/en_US/rwl/search/), which includes the holdings of the following branches: Ayr, Baden, Bloomingdale, Elmira, Linwood, New Dundee, New Hamburg, St. Clements, St. Jacobs, and Wellesley

### Archives:
* _FamilySearch_'s [Canada Archives and Libraries](https://wiki.familysearch.org/en/Canada_Archives_and_Libraries) wiki

### Vendors of new and used books:
* [AbeBooks.com](https://www.abebooks.com/)
* [Amazon.ca](https://www.amazon.ca/)

## Zotero group library

The German Canadiana in Ontario (GCO) Bibliography can now be openly accessed through a [Zotero group library](https://www.zotero.org/groups/6606998/gco/items).

Note: The GCO does not require a Zotero account to view.

### About Zotero

Zotero is a reference manager that collects, manages, and cites the sources you find during your research. Zotero has the capability to export bibliographies according to various citation styles.

## Copyright information

Elizabeth Bloomfield has generously given us permission to extract relevant records from the Waterloo Regional History Bibliography System, a system Dr. Bloomfield created and for which she holds the copyright. These records have greatly enriched the GCO Bibliography. These entries are identified in the "Source" field of the GCO Bibliography records. To discover sources describing all aspects Waterloo Region (pre-1973), consult the work by Elizabeth Bloomfield: Waterloo County to 1972: an Annotated Bibliography of Regional History (with Linda Foster and Jane Forgay). Kitchener: Waterloo Regional Heritage Foundation, 1993.
